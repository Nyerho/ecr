# ECR Privacy Notice Draft

> **Draft for legal and operating-owner review.** This document is not legal advice and must not be published as the final notice without confirming the operator, jurisdiction, lawful basis, contact details, retention schedule, and user-rights process.

## What ECR does

Emergency Community Response (ECR) helps people submit incident reports to participating response organizations. ECR is not a replacement for official emergency services. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate official emergency number first.

## Information collected

Depending on the report and the channel used, ECR may collect a report description, emergency category, name, phone number, email address, approximate or precise location, affected-person count, status events, device notification token, and optional image attachments.

## Why information is used

Information is used to receive, triage, route, assign, update, audit, secure, and resolve incident reports; send status notifications; prevent abuse; investigate service failures; and meet documented operational and legal obligations.

## Who can access it

Public tracking uses a separate sanitized projection. It must not include reporter contact details, full descriptions, precise coordinates, private media paths, or download URLs. Authorized institutional operators may access sensitive incident information only within their role, organization, jurisdiction, and assignment scope. Administrators may access information for security, governance, support, and audit purposes.

## Location and attachments

Location is optional unless the approved reporting flow requires it. Users should avoid approaching danger or photographing uninvolved people. Attachments are stored privately, checked for malware, and made available only after successful verification. Failed or abandoned uploads are removed according to the approved retention policy.

## Retention and deletion requests

ECR retains information only for the approved operational, safety, legal, and accountability period. The final schedule and deletion-request contact must be inserted after legal review. Some audit records may be retained longer to protect service integrity and investigate abuse.
